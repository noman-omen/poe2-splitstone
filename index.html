Desktop

These apply only in the Splitstone Windows/Linux app. Global hotkeys use Ctrl+Alt combos so they do not steal Space/letters from the game.

Checks for a new version when the app starts. Uncheck to stop automatic checks. Global: Ctrl+Alt+S split · Ctrl+Alt+Z undo · Ctrl+Alt+P pause · Ctrl+Alt+R new run · Ctrl+Alt+H show/hide hints · Ctrl+Alt+O lock/unlock overlay.

Global hotkeys

Type a combo such as Ctrl+Alt+S and press Enter. Use Ctrl+Alt or Ctrl+Shift with a letter, digit or F-key.

Saved data

A copy of your save from before 1.1 is kept for a while after the upgrade.

Ghost race

Shows ahead/behind per split against your PB, best segments, a past run or a ghost imported from a friend, plus where the ghost would be now. Not shown on the in-game overlay.
